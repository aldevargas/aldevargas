      <h1>Brazilian Canja Recipe</h1>
      <p>An amazing meal when you are sick or hungover.</p>
      <h2>Ingredients</h2>
      <ul>
        <li>400gr of chicken</li>
        <li>500gr of potatos</li>
        <li>1 onion</li>
        <li>3 garlic cloves</li>
        <li>2 chicken broth tablet</li>
        <li>1 bunch of green onion</li>
        <li>salt</li>
        <li>pepper</li>
      </ul>
      <h2>Instructions</h2>
      <ol>
        <li>Put the chicken, the potatos, the onion, the garlic and the chicken broth with salt and pepper in a pressure cooker</li>
        <li>Cook it for 30 min.</li>
        <li>After finish, way some minutes to reduce the pressure and open the cooker to add green onions</li>
        <li>You can serve it with toasts!</li>
